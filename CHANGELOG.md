# Button Field changelog

This log tracks player-facing changes and save compatibility.

## Unreleased

See the [development plan](DEVELOPMENT_PLAN.md) for proposed work.

## 2026-11-27

- Published the standalone game with five fields and an equipment forge. Added a progression guide and improved save recovery.
- Expanded equipment with crafted sets, evolution, loot automation, item locks, and comparison tools.
- Added farming and mining, with materials that feed recipes and Forge upgrades.
- Added 15 companions with field assignments, training, evolution, artifacts, and expeditions that continue offline.
- Added ten optional field bosses with distinct attack patterns, timed Guard, permanent trophies, and three combat equipment sets. Mid-fight saves and pending gear claims survive reloads.
- Existing saves migrate to schema 5. Settings can export the original local save from before combat migration.
