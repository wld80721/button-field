# Button Field changelog

This log tracks player-facing changes and save compatibility.

## Unreleased

## 2026-11-28 · Navigation and Blacksmith fixes

- Grasslands activities open from their landmarks. Regional fields keep the shortcuts they need, and the Garden and Mine landmarks show their current status.
- The reward wheel uses one return button, and the Boss Board has one field entrance. Crafting and tempering each use one action with a payment selector.
- The Blacksmith opens in place when passive earnings reach its unlock. Short landscape screens keep the anvil and timing meter visible together. The Garden returns to ordinary Glowleaf after you plant your last magical seed.

## 2026-11-27 · The Legendary Blacksmith

- **The Legendary Blacksmith.** Visit the Grasslands Forge to heat, hammer, and quench equipment. Use Timing Assist to shape pieces one strike at a time. Mythic and Celestial evolution reveal a new look at the anvil.
- Find blueprints through the Mine, Garden, Grove Trail expedition, and Mossguard fight. Craft Thunder Gauntlet, Phantom Charm, Nature Boots, or Dragon Ring and specialize with two- and four-piece bonuses. Gear comparisons show how a swap changes active abilities.
- Existing equipment, locks, and loot settings migrate to schema 7. Export the original local save from **Save & settings → Export pre-Blacksmith backup**. Blueprint discoveries carry through Ascension.

## 2026-11-27 · The Living Field

- **The Living Field.** Grasslands grows around the red button with animated flora, wildlife, paths, and landmark construction. Enter illustrated activity scenes from the field and use the trade gate to visit unlocked regions.
- Find three hidden keepsakes with one-time materials. The decorations carry over after Ascension.
- Saves migrate to schema 6 under the existing storage key. Export the original save under **Save & settings → Export pre-Living Field backup**.

## 2026-11-27

- Published the standalone game with five fields and an equipment forge. Added a progression guide and improved save recovery.
- Expanded equipment with crafted sets, evolution, loot automation, item locks, and comparison tools.
- Added farming and mining, with materials that feed recipes and Forge upgrades.
- Added 15 companions with field assignments, training, evolution, artifacts, and expeditions that continue offline.
- Added ten optional field bosses with distinct attack patterns, timed Guard, permanent trophies, and three combat equipment sets. Mid-fight saves and pending gear claims survive reloads.
- Existing saves migrate to schema 5. Settings can export the original local save from before combat migration.
